### Journey after confirmation — classic chat (`/`) and demo mock

Specification: `docs/PLANNER_CONTRACT.md`; design: `docs/DESIGN.md` §14; rules: `apps/web/AGENTS.md`. The `/vocal` side of the same journey is another workstream and is not described here.

**Implemented.**
- `src/services/mockJourneyApi.ts` (demo mode): scripted port of `services/api/app/services/planner.py` and of the journey methods of `services/conversation.py` — same catalogue, ranking, itinerary variants, adjustments (return later 30/60/90, departure earlier, fewer activities, nearer destination, real `unlocks` counts, at most six, smallest change of each kind first), `no_match`, review, booking pending → confirmed or refused once with alternatives, travel file, feedback; scenarios `search_scenario` / `booking_scenario`; errors `502 PLANNER_ERROR`, `502 BOOKING_ERROR`, `409 INVALID_STATE` (also `409 UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY`, `422 INVALID_BODY`, `404 CONVERSATION_NOT_FOUND`) with the backend's French details. It exports `mockJourneyApi` only. Its state is stored inside the demo conversation (`journey` next to the response, same `localStorage` entry) and dropped whenever the demo conversation loses its confirmation; an accepted time adjustment goes through `mockJourneyBridge.applyAcceptedChanges` in `mockConversationApi.ts` (requirements updated, version + 1, still confirmed, the two messages of the backend). Ids are stable but not the backend's (FNV digest instead of SHA-256): `iti_…`, `adj_…` and `SIM-…` values differ from the real API's for the same request. Shifts are computed on wall-clock time: a shift across a daylight-saving change would be off by an hour (not reachable with same-day requests).
- UI, `src/components/chatbot/journey/` (one component per file, co-located tests), rendered by `ChatContainer` under the confirmed card and the JSON block when the conversation is `CONFIRMED` and `config.planner_mode` is `simulated` or `http` (absent = off): start card (+ « Scénario de test », simulated mode only, collapsed), destinations, itineraries, adjustments, no_match, review, booking pending (one automatic check after 2.5 s, then « Vérifier la réponse »), booked + travel file + print, refused + alternatives, feedback, closed. `JourneyNotices` renders `journey.notices` on every view and inside the printed file. `JourneyActions` (« Changer de destination », « Modifier ma demande », « Relancer la recherche ») closes every stage before `booking_pending`.
- `ChatContainer` calls `useJourney` (shared hook, unchanged) with `onRequirementsChanged: chat.refresh`; `ConversationThread` aligns the start of the journey (`data-thread-anchor`) to the top when the view changes; `ConfirmationCard` gets a « Voir la suite : propositions de sortie » button when the journey follows (the start card is under the JSON block, usually below the fold); `JourneyInterruptedNotice` explains why the journey left after an edit. Print styles in `globals.css` (`[data-print-area]`).
- `src/lib/format.ts`: `formatClock`, `formatDay`, `formatDuration`, `formatTimeNoWrap`.

- **Five fields.** `src/types/contracts.ts` mirrors the contract at schema_version 1.1: `FieldName` gains `departure_station`; canonical order `participants_count, departure_station, departure, return, teaching_type` (`FIELD_NAMES`); `requirements.departure_station: string | null`; `RequirementValue<"departure_station"> = string`; `ConfirmedRequirements.schema_version: "1.1"` with a required `departure_station`. `ClientConfig.planner_mode?: "off" | "simulated" | "http"` is typed and returned as `"simulated"` by the mock; `ChatContainer` reads it to decide whether the journey is shown (see above).

The Ajv test validates the mock against `packages/contracts/*.schema.json` and passes with the regenerated schemas.

**Responsive behaviour** (`docs/DESIGN.md` §5 and §13).
- Breakpoints are container queries on the app shell, in rem, declared as Tailwind variants in `globals.css` (`narrow`, `single`, `titled`, `wide`, `roomy`, `spacious`, `short`, `tall`, `low`, `mouse`). Width, height, zoom and text size all go through them; there are no `sm:`/`lg:` layout breakpoints left in the chat UI.
- Two columns from 48rem (768 px) with a 17.5rem panel (22rem from 64rem, 24rem from 80rem); single column below, where the panel is a summary bar whose details open as a layer over the conversation area (never over the composer). Content is centred at 1280 px on wider screens.
- Compact mode from the viewport **height** (≤ 32.5rem): slim top bar, one-line summary, one-row composer, no keyboard hint. Measured conversation height in the welcome state: 279 px (71.5 %) at 844×390, 204 px (54.4 %) at 667×375, 149 px (46.6 %) at 568×320.
- Large text / zoom: rows wrap, buttons grow instead of clipping, long words break, « Aide » / panel toggle / « Modifier » fall back to icon-only with accessible names on narrow shells or panels, confirmation-card buttons stack, and the shell scrolls vertically as a whole when its minimum content does not fit (conversation area never under 8rem). Zoom is not restricted.
- Mobile chrome: `100dvh` with `100vh` fallback, `viewport-fit=cover` + safe-area paddings, `interactive-widget=resizes-content` plus `useKeyboardInset` (visual viewport) for browsers that overlay the keyboard, inputs ≥ 16 px and targets ≥ 44 px on coarse pointers.
- « Revenir en bas » has its own row under the thread (icon-only in a right gutter on phone landscape) and no longer covers text.
- The help dialog opens at its beginning (focus on the dialog, not on « Fermer ») and scrolls inside when taller than the viewport; the transcript field is capped at 7rem and scrolls inside.
